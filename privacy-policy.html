Privacy Policy — PolyPal

Last updated: 2026-10-17 (v1.3.0)

This policy describes what data PolyPal ("the app") collects, stores, and shares. It is written to match the App Store Connect App Privacy nutrition label exactly. If this document and the nutrition label ever disagree, the nutrition label is wrong — let us know at support@medbillresolve.com.

What the app collects

Anonymous product-analytics events sent to PostHog (US region). We use these to understand how the app is used in aggregate and to improve it. Events are kid-safe: event properties are enum strings or numbers only. We do NOT collect:

  • names, email, phone number, address, or any other contact info
  • location — PostHog GeoIP enrichment is explicitly disabled ($geoip_disable=true on every event)
  • device model, device name, or any other hardware identifier
  • code the kid writes, lesson answers, prompts to the AI, or AI responses
  • IDFA / advertising identifiers (none requested)
  • any content from third-party apps

Each install is identified by a random UUID stored in iOS UserDefaults (portfolio.analytics.install_id). That UUID is reset when the app is deleted and reinstalled.

Events we send

Lifecycle (install, session.start, session.end, retention.day_n), onboarding (onboarding.complete with age band / goal / daily goal enums), learning (lesson.start, step.submit with pass/attempt counts, hint.used, streak.*, daily_limit.hit, ai.unavailable, parental-gate pass/fail counts), monetization (paywall.view, paywall.purchase_result, restore.result), and errors (error.shown with enum codes). Full event-property contract is in the app source at PolyPal/Core/Analytics/LearningAnalytics.swift.

What the app stores locally

  • Lesson/step completion IDs (strings, e.g. 'hello#2') stored in iOS UserDefaults (pypal.completedLessonIDs.v2, pypal.passedStepIDs.v2).
  • XP, streak length, freezes, daily checked-step count — all integers.
  • Onboarding answers (age band, goal, daily goal) stored as short enum strings.
  • Certificate first name — only if a parent entered one behind the parental gate. Never transmitted.
  • Analytics install_id and session index described above.

No personally identifying information is stored or transmitted.

The on-device AI tutor ("Ask PolyPal")

Unit 6 lessons and the optional "Ask PolyPal" hint button use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Every prompt, every response, and the full conversation history stay on your device. Nothing is transmitted to Apple, to us, or to anyone else. We log a single hint.used event (level and source enum only) when a kid reveals a hint — never the content.

Permissions we request

  • Notifications — only after a parent passes the parental gate inside Settings → Reminders, and only to deliver the optional daily practice reminder. Off by default.

No camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no motion.

Payments and the parental gate

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the transaction; we never see your Apple ID or payment details. Every purchase sheet, Restore flow, external link (Terms, Privacy, support), notification prompt, certificate share, and reminder toggle is first gated by a short "Ask a grown-up" math question so an accidental kid tap doesn't reach the underlying action.

Ads and cross-app tracking

None. PolyPal shows no ads and does not use IDFA / App Tracking Transparency. We do not track users across apps or websites.

Third-party services

  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics as described above. US region. GeoIP enrichment disabled. Device auto-props stripped before every event is sent.
  • Apple — StoreKit (payments), FoundationModels (local AI), standard iOS frameworks (SwiftUI, UIKit, Foundation, UserNotifications).

Your controls

  • Settings → Opt out of analytics stops all PostHog events at the SDK level.
  • Revoke notification permission in iOS Settings → PolyPal at any time.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally-stored data, including the random install_id.
  • Settings → Reset Progress clears the locally-stored v2 completion and XP/streak data without uninstalling.
  • Contact us at support@medbillresolve.com to request any data we hold or to ask a question about this policy.

Children

PolyPal targets ages 8–14. The app contains no user-generated content visible to other users, no chat with other users, no social features, no leaderboards, no advertising, no in-app purchases of consumables or tokens. The on-device AI is wrapped with age-appropriate system instructions on top of Apple's own guardrails and sends no data off the device.

Changes

We'll update this page when the data we collect changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes (new data types, new third-party services) will also prompt an in-app notice on next launch.
